# Topic 5.3 — Practice & Review

---

## Key Facts to Know Cold

- Spinning jenny: **James Hargreaves, 1760s** — spun multiple threads at once
- Water frame: **Richard Arkwright, 1769** — waterpower; "father of the factory system"
- Interchangeable parts: **Eli Whitney, 1798** → led to division of labor
- Steam engine (improved): **James Watt, 1765** — cheap coal power for factories
- **Enclosure movement** → displaced small farmers → urban factory workers
- Britain's capital for industrialization came partly from **Atlantic slave trade profits**
- Agricultural revolution: **crop rotation + seed drill + potato** → more food → population growth
- Coal deposits in: **South Wales, Yorkshire, Lancashire** (Britain)

## Multiple Choice Practice

**1.** Which of the following best explains why Britain industrialized before France in the late 18th century?

A) Britain had a larger population and thus a bigger consumer market
B) France's government actively opposed technological innovation
C) Britain had greater access to capital, coal, and a displaced rural labor force
D) Britain's democratic government encouraged entrepreneurship more than France's monarchy

*Answer: C — Britain's combination of slave-trade capital, coal deposits, and the enclosure movement's labor supply gave it decisive advantages over France, which also suffered from war-induced capital drain.*

---

**2.** The enclosure movement in Britain contributed most directly to industrialization by:

A) increasing agricultural productivity through crop rotation
B) providing cheap raw materials for textile factories
C) forcing displaced farmers into cities where they became factory workers
D) giving the government revenue to fund railroad construction

*Answer: C — The enclosure movement displaced small farmers who had relied on the commons, pushing them into cities like Manchester and Liverpool where they joined the industrial workforce.*

---

**3.** Eli Whitney's system of interchangeable parts (1798) contributed to the Industrial Revolution primarily by:

A) making it possible for factories to produce steam engines cheaply
B) enabling specialization of labor and mass production of goods
C) replacing the water frame with a more efficient spinning technology
D) providing the U.S. military with weapons superior to those made in Britain

*Answer: B — Whitney's system meant workers no longer needed to craft an entire product — each could specialize in one task, increasing speed and reducing the need for skilled labor.*

## Short Answer Question (SAQ)

**Prompt:** Briefly explain ONE reason why Britain was the first country to industrialize in the period c. 1750–1830.

**Strong response example:** Britain's possession of vast coal deposits was a crucial factor in its early industrialization. Coal powered James Watt's improved steam engine (1765), which provided cheap, mobile energy for textile factories and, within 50 years, for steam-powered trains. Unlike waterpower (which required proximity to rivers), coal could be mined, transported, and burned anywhere — giving British industrialists a flexible, scalable energy source that rivals like France initially lacked.

## Common Misconceptions

> [!warning]
> The Industrial Revolution did NOT begin because of genius inventors working in isolation. It emerged from a specific combination of economic demand (investors wanting faster cloth production), available capital, geographic advantages, and agricultural changes that created surplus labor. Technology responded to these pressures.

> [!warning]
> Don't forget the **slave trade connection**. Britain's industrial capital was partly financed by Atlantic slave trade profits. This also explains why British-grown cotton isn't the story — it was American slave-produced cotton imported to British factories.
