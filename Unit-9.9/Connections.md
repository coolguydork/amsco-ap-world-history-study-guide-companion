# Connections

**Unit 9.9 — Continuity & Change in a Globalized World | AMSCO pp. 707–720**

---

## Builds On

Topic 9.9 is the synthesis capstone for all of Unit 9 — it draws directly on every preceding topic as its content. There are no "leads to" topics; this is the terminal node.

**[[Unit-9.1/Advances in Technology & Exchange Overview\|Advances in Technology & Exchange Overview]]**
The technology advances surveyed in 9.9 (communication, transportation, agricultural, energy) build directly on the detailed case studies in 9.1 — the Internet, containerized shipping, and the Green Revolution are all introduced there.

**[[Unit-9.2/Technological Advancements & Limitations — Disease Overview\|Technological Advancements & Limitations — Disease Overview]]**
The medical science changes in 9.9 (vaccines, antibiotics, life expectancy) and the persistent disease burden in developing nations (cholera, HIV/AIDS documents in the DBQ) synthesize 9.2's analysis of disease and technological response.

**[[Unit-9.3/Technology & the Environment Overview\|Technology & the Environment Overview]]**
The environmental changes and continuities in 9.9 — climate change, pollution, biodiversity loss — are the macro-level synthesis of 9.3's focused treatment of the environment and technology relationship.

**[[Unit-9.4/Economics in the Global Age Overview\|Economics in the Global Age Overview]]**
The economic policy table and the analysis of Asia's rise, China's Four Modernizations, and core-periphery persistence in 9.9 synthesize the globalization economics covered in depth in 9.4.

**[[Unit-9.5/Calls for Reform & Responses Overview\|Calls for Reform & Responses Overview]]**
The political and social changes in 9.9 — women's rights, civil rights movements, anti-apartheid, Arab Spring — synthesize the reform movements detailed in 9.5. AMSCO explicitly cites 9.5 (See Topic 9.5) for birth control and women's status.

**[[Unit-9.6/Globalized Culture Overview\|Globalized Culture Overview]]**
The cultural changes in 9.9 — Western cultural dominance, bidirectional cultural exchange, consumer culture — synthesize 9.6's treatment of cultural globalization.

**[[Unit-9.7/Resistance to Globalization Overview\|Resistance to Globalization Overview]]**
The political and cultural continuities in 9.9 (authoritarianism, cultural resistance, economic inequality) reflect the persistent resistance covered in 9.7 — globalizations benefits were contested throughout.

**[[Unit-9.8/Institutions Developing in a Globalized World Overview\|Institutions Developing in a Globalized World Overview]]**
The international institutions (UN, WHO, UNICEF) that appear in 9.9's DBQ documents were created to manage the very changes described — medical standards, vaccine programs, aging population policy.
