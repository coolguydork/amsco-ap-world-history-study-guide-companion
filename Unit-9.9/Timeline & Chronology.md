## Economic Policy Chronology

| Era | Country | Policy | Direction |
|-----|---------|--------|-----------|
| 1921–28 | USSR | Lenin's NEP | ← market elements |
| 1928–32 | USSR | Stalin's Five-Year Plan | → forced collectivization |
| 1933–41 | USA | Roosevelt's New Deal | → state intervention |
| 1945–51 | UK | Welfare state expansion | → state services |
| 1958–60 | China | Great Leap Forward | → forced industrialization |
| 1970s | China | Deng's Four Modernizations | ← market opening |
| 1980s | USA | Reagan tax cuts | ← deregulation |
| 1980s | UK | Thatcher privatization | ← market primacy |

> [!exam-tip]
> The economic policy pendulum — state intervention ↔ market liberalization — is a key CCOT pattern. Note that **both communist and capitalist states** intervened heavily in their economies at different moments, qualifying any simple capitalism-vs-communism narrative.
