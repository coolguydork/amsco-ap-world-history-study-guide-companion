# Timeline & Chronology

> [!tip]
> The Atlantic revolutions cascaded — each one inspired the next. Notice how tightly 1776 → 1789 → 1791 are linked. For the European unification movements, Bismarck's three wars (1864, 1866, 1870–71) run in rapid succession and are frequently tested.

## Master Chronology

| Date | Event | Significance |
|------|-------|--------------|
| 1776 | American Declaration of Independence (July 4) | Applied Enlightenment ideals; first successful colonial revolution against a European power |
| 1783 | American Revolution ends | U.S. victory with crucial French military and financial aid; model for later Atlantic revolutions |
| 1789 | French Revolution begins | Estates-General meets; Bastille stormed July 14; Declaration of Rights of Man adopted |
| 1791 | Haitian slave revolt begins | Toussaint L'Ouverture leads formerly enslaved Africans and Maroons; only successful slave revolution in history |
| 1792 | First French Republic established | Radical Jacobins take power; Reign of Terror follows; king executed 1793 |
| 1801 | L'Ouverture produces Haitian constitution | Grants equality and citizenship to all; declares himself governor-for-life; initiates land reform |
| 1803 | Toussaint L'Ouverture dies in French captivity | Betrayed and imprisoned by Napoleon; successor Dessalines completes independence |
| 1804 | Haiti declares permanent independence | First Black republic; only country born from a successful slave uprising |
| 1804 | Napoleon becomes Emperor of France | Spreads then undermines revolutionary ideals; conquests paradoxically fuel European nationalism |
| 1815 | Congress of Vienna settles Napoleonic Wars | Conservative restoration in Europe; Bolívar's "Jamaica Letter" articulates Latin American independence goals |
| 1827 | British, French, Russian fleet destroys Ottoman fleet | Secures Greek independence — first Balkan nation to break from Ottoman control |
| 1840 | Britain formally annexes New Zealand | Intensifies pressure on Maori land; triggers New Zealand Wars (conclude ~1872) |
| 1848 | Revolutions sweep Europe | Nationalist and liberal uprisings in France, Prussia, Austria-Hungary; mostly suppressed, but accelerate nationalist movements |
| 1858 | Cavour maneuvers France against Austria | Key diplomatic step in Italian unification via realpolitik |
| 1864 | Seven Weeks' War (Austro-Prussian War) begins with Denmark war | Bismarck's first war; Prussia and Austria vs. Denmark — Prussia gains Schleswig |
| 1866 | Seven Weeks' War (Austro-Prussian War) | Prussia defeats Austria; Bismarck dominates German states; Austria excluded from German affairs |
| 1868 | Uprisings in Puerto Rico and Cuba begin | Nationalist challenges to Spain's last major Caribbean colonial holdings; Lola Rodríguez de Tió exiled |
| 1870–71 | Franco-Prussian War | Prussia defeats France; Bismarck wins Alsace-Lorraine; final step in German unification |
| 1871 | German Empire founded | Bismarck unifies Germany under Prussian leadership; King William I becomes Kaiser; reshapes European balance of power |
| 1872 | New Zealand Wars end | British victory; Maori land losses; Maori national identity persists |
| 1892 | José Rizal arrested in Philippines | Propaganda Movement suppressed; Rizal's arrest and execution (1896) transforms autonomy movement into revolution |
| 1896 | Philippine Revolution begins | First major nationalist military challenge to Spanish colonial rule in Asia; inspired by Enlightenment and European nationalism |

## Thematic Groupings

**Atlantic Revolutions (cascading influence)**
1776 (American) → 1789 (French) → 1791 (Haitian) → c. 1810s–1820s (Latin American)

**Anti-colonial challenges to Spain**
1868 (Puerto Rico & Cuba uprisings) → 1892–1896 (Philippine Propaganda Movement → Revolution)

**European [[Concept Notes/Nationalism\|Nationalism]] and Unification**
1827 (Greek independence) → 1848 (revolutions) → 1858 (Italian realpolitik) → 1864/1866/1870–71 (Bismarck's three wars) → 1871 (German Empire)

**New Zealand**
1840 (annexation) → 1840–1872 (New Zealand Wars) → 1872 (British victory)
