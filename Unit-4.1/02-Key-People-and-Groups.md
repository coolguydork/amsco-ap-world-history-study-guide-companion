# Key People and Groups — Unit 4.1: (4.1): Technological Innovations

#apwh #unit-4 #period-4

---

### Prince Henry the Navigator (1394–1460)
- **Region:** Portugal
- **Period:** Early–mid 15th century
- Portuguese royal patron who funded systematic maritime exploration of the West African coast, establishing a school of navigation at Sagres.
- Sponsored the development and testing of new ship designs and navigational instruments, creating an institutional framework for technological innovation.
- His model of state-sponsored exploration became the template for later Spanish, Dutch, and English ventures.

---

### Johannes Gutenberg (c. 1400–1468)
- **Region:** Holy Roman Empire (Germany)
- **Period:** Mid 15th century
- Developed the movable-type [[Printing press]] around 1440, revolutionizing the production and distribution of books, maps, and scientific texts in Europe.
- His press enabled the rapid diffusion of geographical knowledge, navigational charts, and classical texts that informed European exploration.
- Indirectly enabled the Protestant Reformation by making the Bible and theological arguments widely accessible.

---

### Arab/Islamic Scholars and Navigators
- **Region:** Middle East, North Africa, Indian Ocean
- **Period:** 8th–15th centuries
- Transmitted classical Greek astronomical and geographical texts to Europe, including Ptolemy's *Geography*.
- Developed and refined the astrolabe, lateen sail, and dhow ship design, technologies later adapted by Portuguese explorers.
- Arab and Swahili merchants had mastered Indian Ocean navigation using monsoon winds for centuries before European arrival.

---

### Chinese Inventors and the Ming Navy
- **Region:** China
- **Period:** Song through Ming dynasties (9th–15th centuries)
- Chinese inventors developed gunpowder (9th century), the magnetic compass (11th century), and stern rudder technology.
- Admiral [[Zheng He]]'s voyages (1405–1433) demonstrated the potential of state-sponsored maritime exploration but were discontinued — a key counterfactual for AP discussions.
- China's technologies diffused westward through Mongol-era trade networks and Islamic intermediaries.

---

### Italian Merchants and Financiers (Florence, Venice, Genoa)
- **Region:** Italian city-states
- **Period:** 13th–15th centuries
- Developed double-entry bookkeeping (Luca Pacioli codified it in 1494), bills of exchange, and early banking systems.
- Provided the commercial infrastructure and financial instruments later adopted by Atlantic trading powers.
- Genoese financiers (including Columbus himself) were central to the early Spanish colonial enterprise.

> [!exam-tip]
> **Zheng He** is a high-frequency comparison point on the AP exam. Be ready to explain why China *did not* sustain maritime expansion (Confucian court politics, cost, agricultural focus) versus why Portugal *did* — and what the consequences of each choice were.
