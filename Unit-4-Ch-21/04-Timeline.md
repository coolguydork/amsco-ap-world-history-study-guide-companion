# Timeline — Chapter 21 (4.8): Continuity and Change — Unit 4 Master Timeline

#apwh #unit-4 #period-4

> [!tip] Big Picture
> This is a synthesis timeline for the entire Unit 4 period (1450–1750). Use it to review the full arc of the unit and to identify where major turning points occurred — critical for CCOT essays.

|------|--------|-------|--------------------------|
| 1415 | Portugal captures Ceuta; systematic exploration begins | 4.1, 4.2 | **CHANGE**: State-sponsored oceanic exploration begins |

| 1519–1521 | Cortés conquers Aztec Empire | 4.4 | **CHANGE**: Indigenous imperial states destroyed; colonial hierarchy begins |

> [!warning] CCOT Reminder
> For AP essays, change and continuity must **both be addressed**. A response that only identifies changes — even if accurate — will not earn the highest scores. Practice identifying a continuity for every change you name.
