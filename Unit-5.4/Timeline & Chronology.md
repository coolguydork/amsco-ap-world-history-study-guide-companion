## AP Period Context

Topic 5.4 falls within **AP Period 3 (c. 1750–1900)**. The arc in economic/industrial history:

- **1750–1800**: Britain industrializes; India and Egypt remain major manufacturing powers
- **1800–1850**: Belgium and France begin industrializing; colonial policies deindustrialize India and Egypt; Japan isolated
- **1850–1871**: USA expands; Germany fragmented; Perry forces Japan open
- **1871–1900**: Germany, Russia, and Japan industrialize rapidly via state direction; European share of global manufacturing dominates; India's mining industry abandoned
