## Chronology of the Mongol Empire

| Date | Event | Significance |
|------|-------|--------------|
| **1162** | Temujin (Genghis Khan) born | Future conqueror will build the largest contiguous land empire in history |
| **1204** | Uyghur scribe captured; adapts alphabet for Mongol | First written Mongol script; Uyghur alphabet still used in Mongolia today |
| **1206** | Temujin elected Great Khan at kuriltai; takes name **Genghis Khan** | Mongol unification complete; systematic conquest of Eurasia begins |
| **1210** | Mongols attack Jin Empire (northern China) | First major conquest; reputation for brutal retribution spreads |
| **1219** | Conquest of Kara Khitai and Khwarazm Empires (Central Asia/Persia) | Empire reaches eastern Persia; Silk Roads come under Mongol control |
| **1227** | Genghis Khan dies | Empire stretches from North China Sea to eastern Persia |
| **1236** | **Batu Khan** leads Golden Horde into Russia | Russia subjugated; indirect rule via tribute through Russian princes |
| **1240** | Kiev looted and destroyed | Russia's cultural capital devastated; Golden Horde dominates Eastern Europe |
| **1241** | Battle with Polish/German/French knights; Ögedei Khan dies | Batu withdraws from Western Europe to attend funeral — saves Western Europe from conquest |
| **1258** | **Hulagu Khan** destroys Baghdad; Abbasid Caliphate ends | House of Wisdom burned; perhaps 200,000 killed; most devastating Mongol conquest |
| **c. 1250** | **Pax Mongolica** begins | Trade revitalized; Silk Roads at their third golden age; cultural exchange peaks |
| **1260** | Battle of Ain Jalut — Mamluks defeat Mongols in Egypt | First major Mongol defeat; defines western limit of Mongol expansion |
| **1260** | Kublai Khan becomes Great Khan | Begins conquest of Song China; adapts Chinese governance |
| **1271** | **Kublai Khan** establishes **Yuan Dynasty** in China | China integrated into Mongol Empire; Marco Polo's journey made possible |
| **1279** | Yuan Dynasty completes conquest of Song China | Mongol rule spans world's largest economy |
| **1274** | Failed attempt to conquer Japan (first) | Storms destroy fleet; weakens Mongol prestige in China |
| **c. 1350** | Pax Mongolica ends; Black Death peaks in Europe | Same trade networks that enabled commerce spread bubonic plague |
| **1350s** | **White Lotus Society** organizes resistance in China | Secret Buddhist organization; lays groundwork for anti-Mongol revolt |
| **1368** | **Zhu Yuanzhang** founds **Ming Dynasty**; Mongols expelled | Yuan Dynasty collapses; Ming reverses Mongol policies; restores civil service exam |
| **1380** | Battle of Kulikovo — Russia defeats Golden Horde | Mongol decline in Russia begins; Moscow emerges as center of Russian state |
| **c. 1369** | **Tamerlane** conquers Central Asian territories | Chagatai Khanate and remaining Central Asian Mongol power absorbed |

## Phases of Mongol History

**Phase 1 — Unification (before 1206)**
Temujin consolidates fractured Mongol clans through alliance, marriage diplomacy, and conquest of rival tribes.

**Phase 2 — Conquest Under Genghis Khan (1206–1227)**
Systematic campaigns east (Jin Empire/northern China), then west (Central Asia, Persia). Empire established through terror and military genius.

**Phase 3 — Expansion Under Successors (1227–1260)**
Ögedei, Batu, and Hulagu extend the empire into Russia, Eastern Europe, and the Islamic heartlands. Destruction of Baghdad (1258) and near-conquest of Europe. First major defeat at Ain Jalut (1260) sets western limit.

**Phase 4 — The Pax Mongolica (c. 1250–c. 1350)**
Unified Mongol control enables the third golden age of Silk Roads trade. Cultural exchange peaks. Marco Polo travels China. Bubonic plague begins spreading along trade routes.

**Phase 5 — Fragmentation and Decline (c. 1350–1502)**
The [[Concept Notes/Black Death\|Black Death]] disrupts trade and depopulates territories. Chinese resistance builds under Yuan rule. [[Concept Notes/Ming Dynasty\|Ming Dynasty]] expels [[Concept Notes/Mongols\|Mongols]] (1368). Battle of Kulikovo ends Golden Horde's dominance of Russia (1380). Tamerlane absorbs Central Asia.

---

> [!exam-tip]
> Know the sequence: Genghis Khan unifies (1206) → conquests expand → Baghdad destroyed (1258) → Ain Jalut stops westward march (1260) → Kublai founds Yuan Dynasty (1271) → Pax Mongolica → plague spreads → Ming expelled Mongols (1368). These are the most exam-testable dates.
