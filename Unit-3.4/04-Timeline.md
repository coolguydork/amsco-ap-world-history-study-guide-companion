# Unit 3.4: Timeline — Comparison in Land-Based Empires

#apwh #unit-3 #period-2

> **Big picture:** This timeline places the six major land-based empires side by side across c. 1450–1750, revealing that their rise was not coincidental — all were enabled by the same gunpowder military revolution and faced the same administrative challenge of governing large, ethnically and religiously diverse populations. The period opens with the Ottoman conquest of Constantinople and closes with Qing consolidation of a multi-ethnic empire stretching from the Pacific to Central Asia. In between, empires rose and fell based on their ability to manage succession, maintain military advantage, and extract sufficient revenue to sustain both warfare and monumental state-building.
