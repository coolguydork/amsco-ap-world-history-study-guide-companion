# Timeline — Unit 4.6: (4.6): Internal and External Challenges to State Power

#apwh #unit-4 #period-4

> [!tip] Big Picture
> Topic 4.6 runs parallel to 4.4 and 4.5 — resistance and competition were not later developments but immediate responses to empire. Every colonial system created its own opposition from the moment of its establishment.

---

| Date | Event | Region | Significance |
|------|--------|--------|--------------|
| 1519–1521 | Tlaxcalans ally with Cortés against Aztecs | Mesoamerica | Indigenous peoples used Spanish arrival to settle old scores — complex agency, not simple victimhood |
| 1540s | Mixtón War: Indigenous rebellion against Spanish in western New Spain | Mesoamerica | Early large-scale Indigenous resistance to Spanish colonial rule |
| 1577–1580 | Francis Drake's circumnavigation; raids on Spanish ports | Global / Pacific | England challenges Spanish oceanic dominance through state-sponsored privateering |
| c. 1600 | Quilombo dos Palmares established in Brazil | Brazil | Largest maroon community in history; survives ~100 years |
| 1609 | Spain signs peace treaty with maroons in Veracruz | Mexico | First formal recognition of maroon community rights in Spanish Americas |
| 1641 | Dutch capture Malacca from Portugal | Southeast Asia | Dutch commercial competition successfully displaces Portugal in key trade zone |
| 1655 | England seizes Jamaica from Spain | Caribbean | English begin building Caribbean plantation empire at Spanish expense |
| 1680 | Pueblo Revolt drives Spanish from New Mexico | North America | Most successful Indigenous revolt against Spanish colonial rule; 12 years of freedom |
| 1692 | Spanish reconquer New Mexico with modified policies | North America | Spain moderates religious and labor demands — resistance achieves real concessions |
| 1739 | First Maroon Treaty in Jamaica | Caribbean | British colonial government formally recognizes Maroon autonomy in Jamaica |
| 1756–1763 | Seven Years' War reshuffles colonial empires | Global | Britain gains Canada, Florida, India; France loses major colonies; Spain weakened |
| 1780–1781 | Tupac Amaru II rebellion in the Andes | South America | Largest pre-independence revolt in Latin America; 100,000+ casualties; suppressed but forces reforms |

---

> [!warning] Continuity Note
> Resistance to colonial rule was **continuous and immediate** — it did not begin after independence movements. From the first years of colonization, Indigenous peoples, enslaved Africans, and eventually creole elites challenged imperial authority in various forms.
