# Timeline & Chronology — Topic 1.4

*AMSCO pp. 33–42 | Period 1: c. 1200–c. 1450*

## Chronological Overview

| Date | Event | Significance |
|---|---|---|
| c. 1500–400 BCE | **Olmec civilization** (Mesoamerica) | Earliest major Mesoamerican civilization; established feathered serpent deity, step pyramids, ball courts — inherited by later cultures |
| c. 250–900 CE | **Classic Maya period** | Peak of Maya city-state development; greatest achievements in astronomy, calendar, writing |
| c. 700–800s CE | **Mississippian culture emerges** | First large-scale North American civilization; mound-building; matrilineal society |
| c. 850–1150 | **Chaco culture** (Southwest) | Multi-room stone housing; sophisticated water management in arid climate |
| c. 1100 CE | **Cahokia at peak** | Largest pre-Columbian city north of Mexico; comparable in size to many European cities |
| c. 1200s | **Aztec (Mexica) migrate** south into central Mexico | Hunter-gatherers from the north settle in the Valley of Mexico |
| c. 1200s | **Maya city-states continue** into AP Period 1 | Classic period ended; city-states still active through Period 1; Spanish contact begins 1517 |
| c. 1200–1300 | **Mesa Verde cliff dwellings** at peak | Multi-story cliff dwellings in Colorado; declined as climate dried late 13th century |
| **1325** | Aztec found **Tenochtitlan** | Capital city on island in Lake Texcoco; grows to ~200,000 — one of world's largest cities |
| **1428** | **Aztec Triple Alliance** formed | Alliance of Tenochtitlan, Texcoco, Tlacopan creates the Aztec Empire |
| c. **1438** | **Pachacuti** begins conquests | Inca expansion begins; Tawantinsuyu emerges as the largest empire in the Americas |
| c. **1440–1469** | **Moctezuma I** rules Aztecs | Empire expansion; tribute system matures; Aztec at height of power |
| **1450** | **Cahokia abandoned** | Within AP Period 1; Mississippian decline underway — likely flood/crop failure |
| **1402–1472** | **Nezahualcoyotl** — Aztec poet-king of Texcoco | Cultural peak of Aztec civilization; demonstrates intellectual sophistication |
| **1517** | Spanish first contact with Maya | End of Maya autonomy begins |
| **1519** | Hernán Cortés arrives in Mexico | Spanish begin conquest of Aztec Empire; subject peoples ally with Spain |
| **1521** | **Aztec Empire falls** | Tenochtitlan falls to Spanish-indigenous alliance; end of the Triple Alliance |
| c. **1532** | Francisco Pizarro arrives in Inca Empire | Arrives during Inca civil war of succession |
| **1533** | **Inca Empire falls** | Spanish conquer Cuzco; Tawantinsuyu ends (though resistance continues to 1572) |

## Key AP Period 1 Focus (c. 1200–c. 1450)

During the AP exam's Period 1 window, note what's **active** vs. **declining**:
- **Active:** Aztec Triple Alliance (rising), Inca under Pachacuti (expanding), Maya city-states (continuing), Mississippian (declining — Cahokia abandoned 1450)
- **Declining:** Chaco and Mesa Verde (both gone before 1300), Cahokia (abandoned by 1450)
- **Not yet:** Spanish contact (1492+), Aztec/Inca fall (1521/1533)

> [!exam-tip]
> For Period 1 AP questions, focus on the **rise** of the Aztec and Inca empires and their governing structures, not their fall (which belongs to Period 4). The decline of Cahokia (c. 1450) is the one notable collapse *within* the period.

> [!tip]
> **Memory anchor — Inca:** "Pachacuti 1438, Pizarro 1532." A century of empire. The Inca built the largest pre-Columbian empire in less than 100 years — remarkable speed.
>
> **Memory anchor — Aztec:** "Tenochtitlan 1325, Triple Alliance 1428, Fall 1521." Nearly 200 years from founding the capital to the Spanish conquest.
