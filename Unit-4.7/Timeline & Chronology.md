# Timeline & Chronology

## Key Dates

| Date | Event | Significance |
|------|-------|--------------|
| 1290 | Jews expelled from England | Early pattern of European Jewish persecution; precursor to 1492 |
| 1394 | Jews expelled from France | Continued exclusion of Jews from Western Europe |
| 1492 | Edict of Expulsion — Jews expelled from Spain | Ferdinand and Isabella; largest Jewish community in Europe dispersed; many resettled in Ottoman Empire as Sephardic Jews |
| 1492 | Columbus's first voyage | Opens Atlantic; creates conditions for casta system and colonial social hierarchies |
| 1453 | Ottoman conquest of Constantinople | Mehmed II consolidates power; later invites Jewish refugees to Istanbul |
| 1526 | Mughal Empire founded (Babur) | New imperial order in South Asia; sets stage for Akbar's reforms |
| 1547 | Ivan IV becomes first tsar of Russia | Intensifies centralization; crushes Novgorod boyar independence |
| c. 1550 | Roxelana at height of influence | Ottoman harem politics; illustrates women's indirect political power |
| c. 1550s–1600s | Casta system codified in Spanish colonies | Race formalized as legal social category in the Americas |
| 1556–1605 | Akbar the Great rules Mughal Empire | Abolishes jizya; promotes religious tolerance; Din-i-Ilahi |
| 1565 | Ivan IV's Oprichnina campaign begins | Terrorizes boyar class; seizes lands; hallmark of tsarist autocracy |
| c. 1600 | Louis XIV (1638–1715) era approaches | Centralizes France; Versailles as political tool; nobility neutralized |
| 1644 | Qing Dynasty established (Shunzhi Emperor) | Manchu minority rules Han majority; queue requirement enforced |
| 1645 | Jiangyin Massacre (Liu Liangzuo) | 70,000–100,000 Han killed for resisting the queue |
| 1645 | Jiading Massacre (Li Chengdong) | Three massacres in one month enforcing Qing cultural submission |
| 17th c. | Enlightenment begins to reduce European anti-Jewish prejudice | Jews gain greater economic roles in banking and commerce; Netherlands especially tolerant |

## Chronological Patterns

**Early period (1450–1520):** Social hierarchies in [[Concept Notes/Gunpowder Empires|gunpowder empires]] form and stabilize. Ottoman millet system matures. Mughal Empire not yet founded. Spanish colonialism begins restructuring American societies. Jewish expulsions from Spain create Sephardic diaspora.

**Middle period (1520–1620):** Akbar's reign represents peak of Mughal religious tolerance. Casta system codified in Spanish Americas. Ivan IV crushes Russian boyar independence. Ottoman harem politics at its height (Roxelana).

**Late period (1620–1750):** Qing conquest imposes ethnic hierarchy on China. Louis XIV neutralizes French nobility. European Enlightenment begins softening anti-Jewish attitudes. Casta system entrenched and formalized through *casta paintings*.

> [!tip]
> Remember: 1492 is the most important single year in this topic — it marks both the Spanish expulsion of Jews AND Columbus's first voyage, two events that reshape social hierarchies on different continents for the next 300 years.

> [!exam-tip]
> The AP exam often uses dates to test causation. Know these chains: Columbus (1492) → [[Concept Notes/Atlantic Slave Trade|Atlantic Slave Trade]] → Columbian Exchange demographic collapse → casta system → colonial racial hierarchy. Akbar (1556–1605) → Mughal tolerance → jizya abolished → Hindu-Muslim cooperation → Mughal stability.
