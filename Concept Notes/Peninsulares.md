# Peninsulares

People born in Spain (or Portugal) who held the highest social and political positions in the colonial Americas. As the only group considered fully "pure" in the colonial racial hierarchy, peninsulares monopolized top offices — viceroys, bishops, judges — and were distrusted by the creole class born in the colonies.

> [!exam-tip]
> Peninsulares vs. creoles is a classic AP comparison. Both were of European descent, but peninsulares were born in Europe and held official power; creoles were born in the Americas and were excluded from top offices. This resentment drove creole-led independence movements in the early 1800s.

> [!tip]
> Think of the hierarchy as a pyramid: peninsulares → creoles → mestizos → indigenous/enslaved Africans. Each rung reflects both ancestry and birthplace.
