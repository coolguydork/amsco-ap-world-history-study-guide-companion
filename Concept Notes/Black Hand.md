# Black Hand

Serbian nationalist secret society (formally *Ujedinjenje ili smrt* — "Union or Death") founded in 1911 by Serbian military officers. Its goal was to unite all South Slavic peoples under Serbian rule by ending Austro-Hungarian and Ottoman control in the Balkans through violent means if necessary.

**Key role in WWI:** Black Hand members, including Gavrilo Princip, organized and carried out the assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand in Sarajevo on June 28, 1914 — the immediate trigger of World War I. From Serbia's perspective, the Black Hand represented patriotic resistance to foreign occupation; from Austria-Hungary's perspective, it was a terrorist organization sponsored by a hostile state.

**AP significance:** The Black Hand illustrates how extreme nationalist organizations operating within or alongside a state could draw great powers into conflict. It appears in AP context primarily as evidence for **nationalism** as a cause of WWI (the N in MAIN).
