# Conquistadores

Spanish and Portuguese conquerors who led military expeditions in the Americas from the late 15th through 17th centuries, overthrowing powerful indigenous empires — most notably the Aztec (1521) and Inca (1532) — and claiming vast territories for the Spanish Crown. They were motivated by the promise of gold, glory, and spreading Christianity (the "three Gs"). Their conquests triggered the Columbian Exchange, the collapse of indigenous populations, and the establishment of the colonial *encomienda* system.

> [!exam-tip]
> Conquistadores are a cause, not just an event. On any LEQ or DBQ about the Columbian Exchange or colonial social hierarchies, lead with them as the mechanism of conquest that made everything else possible — demographic collapse, forced labor, racial mixing, and the *casta* system all flow from their campaigns.

> [!warning]
> Don't confuse conquistadores with colonists. Conquistadores were military adventurers acting under Crown authorization (*capitulación*); permanent settlers and administrators came later.
