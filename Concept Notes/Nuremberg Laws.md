# Nuremberg Laws

1935 German racial laws that stripped Jews of citizenship, forbade marriage between Jews and non-Jews, and systematically defined "Jewishness" by ancestry. They were the legal foundation of Nazi anti-Semitic persecution — transforming discrimination from social prejudice into state policy and paving the way for the Holocaust.

> [!exam-tip]
> The Nuremberg Laws are key evidence for the AP argument that the Holocaust was a deliberate, state-directed process — not spontaneous mob violence. They show the escalation from legal exclusion (1935) → forced emigration (1938) → genocide (1941–1945). Use this escalation ladder in DBQ and LEQ responses about causes or nature of the Holocaust.

> [!warning]
> Don't stop at "they were racist laws." The AP wants you to explain *mechanism*: the laws bureaucratized racism, making persecution routine, enforceable, and harder to resist or escape.
