# Mestizos

People of mixed European and indigenous American ancestry in colonial Spanish America; emerged as the largest social group in many colonies by the 18th century. Mestizos occupied the middle rungs of the *casta* system — above indigenous and enslaved Africans, below creoles and [[Concept Notes/Peninsulares\|peninsulares]] — and worked as artisans, small merchants, and soldiers.

> [!exam-tip]
> Mestizos are evidence that the colonial *casta* system was biological as well as social. The AP expects you to recognize how racial mixing created new social categories unique to the Americas — distinct from both European and pre-Columbian structures. They also appear in independence-era essays as part of the coalition that challenged peninsulare power.

> [!tip]
> Parallel term: *mulatos* (mixed African and European ancestry). Both groups illustrate how colonial contact zones created entirely new social identities with no pre-contact equivalent.
