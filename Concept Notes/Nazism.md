# Nazism

The ideology and political movement of the National Socialist German Workers' Party (NSDAP), which ruled Germany 1933–1945 under Adolf Hitler.

**Why it matters for AP:** Nazism is the direct cause of WWII in Europe and the Holocaust. Understanding its ideology — what it claimed, why it appealed to millions, and what it did — is essential for AP causation and continuity/change arguments.

## Core Ideology

- **Aryan racial supremacy** — pseudoscientific belief in a Germanic "master race" superior to Jews, Slavs, Roma, and others; rooted in scientific racism and Social Darwinism
- **Anti-Semitism** — extreme, eliminationist hostility to Jews, whom Hitler blamed for Germany's economic problems and national humiliation
- **Lebensraum** — "living space"; Germany must conquer Eastern Europe to provide territory and resources for the Aryan people
- **Ultranationalism** — repudiation of the Treaty of Versailles; restoration of German greatness; total subordination of the individual to the Volk (people/nation)
- **Totalitarian state** — single-party rule, censorship, surveillance, propaganda, elimination of all opposition
