## Chronological Patterns

Three empires collapsed in roughly the same period: **Russia** (1917), **China** (1911), and the **Ottoman Empire** (1918–1923). All three were large land-based empires weakened by internal stagnation and external pressure. Mexico is a different pattern — a nominally independent republic whose authoritarian government was overthrown, not an empire. But Mexico shares the internal-weakness + external-domination dynamic.

WWI (1914–1918) was the key **external accelerant** across multiple cases. It toppled the Russian tsar (1917), destroyed the Ottoman Empire (1918), and contributed to instability in China by distracting European powers who might otherwise have intervened more in Chinese affairs. Mexico's revolution predated WWI but was shaped by U.S. economic and military pressure throughout the period.

> [!tip]
> **AP chronology trick:** Remember "1911, 1917, 1923" for Chinese, Russian, and Turkish revolutions respectively. Mexico's is messiest — starts 1910, constitution 1917, PRI 1929.
