# Timeline & Chronology

**Topic 6.3 — Indigenous Responses to State Expansion | AMSCO pp. 388–397**

---

## Chronological Overview

| Date | Event | AP Significance |
|------|-------|-----------------|
| 1763 | [[Concept Notes/Proclamation of 1763\|Proclamation of 1763]] — Britain reserves land west of Appalachians for Native Americans | First European recognition of indigenous territorial rights; ignored by colonists |
| 1780–1781 | [[Concept Notes/Túpac Amaru II\|Túpac Amaru II]] revolt in Peru | Last major Indian revolt against Spain; spreads to Bolivia and Argentina; crushed |
| 1804–1808 | Usman dan Fodio's jihad → Sokoto Caliphate founded | Largest 19th-century African empire; religious reform + political resistance |
| 1815 | Serbia achieves autonomy from Ottoman Empire | First Balkan nationalist success; inspires future independence movements |
| 1823–1900 | Five Anglo-Asante Wars in West Africa | Asante Empire resists British annexation; finally annexed after Yaa Asantewaa War (1900) |
| 1829 | Greek independence from Ottoman Empire | Major victory for Balkan nationalism; signals Ottoman decline in Europe |
| 1830 | Indian Removal Act (U.S.) | Forces Cherokee and other tribes to Oklahoma; Trail of Tears follows; assimilation fails indigenous peoples |
| 1840 | Treaty of Waitangi — Britain promises Maori land rights | Repeatedly violated; Maori Wars follow; core example of settler colonialism |
| 1840s–1872 | Maori Wars in New Zealand | Maori resist British settler encroachment; lose most land by 1900 |
| 1857 | Indian Rebellion of 1857 (Sepoy Mutiny) | East India Company abolished; British Raj established; Indian nationalism born |
| 1858–1947 | British Raj governs India | British-educated Indians found INC (1885); Indian nationalism grows toward independence |
| 1868–1898 | Samory Touré resists French expansion in West Africa | Sustained military resistance; captured and exiled 1898 |
| 1877–1878 | Russo-Turkish War; Treaty of Berlin | Bulgaria, Romania, Montenegro freed from Ottoman rule; Bosnia to Austria-Hungary |
| 1879–1881 | Anglo-Zulu War | Zulu defeat British at Isandlwana (1879); eventually defeated; Zululand annexed |
| 1881–1898 | Mahdist revolt and Mahdist Sudan | Muhammad Ahmad declares himself Mahdi; captures Khartoum (1885); Mahdist state crushed at Omdurman (1898) |
| 1885 | [[Concept Notes/Indian National Congress\|Indian National Congress]] founded | British-educated Indians organize for self-rule; precursor to independence movement |
| 1885 | Battle of Khartoum; death of General Gordon | Mahdist forces kill British general; shocks British public; elevates Muhammad Ahmad's reputation |
| 1889–1890 | Ghost Dance movement spreads across Great Plains | Spiritual resistance among Native Americans; ends with Wounded Knee Massacre (1890) |
| 1892 | José Rizal founds Liga Filipina | Peaceful reform movement in Philippines; Spanish immediately suppress it |
| 1896 | Battle of Adwa — Ethiopia defeats Italy | Only African nation to defeat a European colonizer during Scramble for Africa; Ethiopia stays independent |
| 1896 | Rizal executed; Philippine Revolution begins | Colonial repression triggers revolution; Emilio Aguinaldo leads revolt |
| 1898 | Spanish-American War; Treaty of Paris | U.S. acquires Philippines; Filipino independence expectations are betrayed |
| 1899–1902 | Philippine-American War | Filipinos resist U.S. colonization; ~20,000 troops + 200,000+ civilians killed |
| 1900–1901 | Yaa Asantewaa War in Gold Coast | Last major African war led by a woman; Golden Stool never surrendered; Asante annexed |
| 1903 | British conquer Sokoto Caliphate | Incorporated into Nigeria; largest 19th-century African empire ends |

## Sequencing by Region

**Americas**
1763 → Proclamation of 1763 (British recognize indigenous land)
1780–1781 → Túpac Amaru II revolt (last great Inca revolt)
1830 → Indian Removal Act (Trail of Tears)
1889–1890 → Ghost Dance movement → Wounded Knee

**South Asia**
1857 → Indian Rebellion (Sepoy Mutiny) → Raj established
1885 → Indian National Congress founded

**Southeast Asia**
1892 → Liga Filipina (Rizal)
1896 → Philippine Revolution
1898 → Treaty of Paris (Philippines to U.S.)
1899–1902 → Philippine-American War

**Balkans**
1815 → Serbian autonomy
1829 → Greek independence
1877–1878 → Russo-Turkish War → Treaty of Berlin frees more Balkan states

**Africa**
1804 → Sokoto Caliphate founded
1823–1900 → Anglo-Asante Wars
1840–1872 → Maori Wars (New Zealand)
1856–1857 → Xhosa Cattle Killing
1868–1898 → Samory Touré resists France
1879 → Anglo-Zulu War (Isandlwana)
1881–1898 → Mahdist Sudan
1885 → Khartoum falls
1896 → Battle of Adwa (Ethiopia)
1898 → Omdurman (British retake Sudan)
1900–1901 → Yaa Asantewaa War

3. **1885** — Indian National Congress founded; Khartoum falls
4. **1896** — Battle of Adwa; Philippine Revolution begins
5. **1899–1902** — Philippine-American War
