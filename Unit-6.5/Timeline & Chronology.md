# Timeline & Chronology

**Topic 6.5 — Economic [[Concept Notes/Imperialism\|Imperialism]] | AMSCO pp. 407–416**

> [!exam-tip]
> The AP exam frequently requires you to place economic imperialism within a chronological sequence. The Opium Wars (1839–1842, 1856–1860) and Treaty of Nanking (1842) are the most tested specific dates in this topic. Also know that British informal empire in Latin America peaked in the **late 19th century** and US economic imperialism in Central America emerged in the **1890s–1900s**.

|------|-------|-----------------|

| 1823 | US Monroe Doctrine | Declares Latin America in US sphere of influence; keeps Europeans out while enabling US corporate dominance |

| 1839 | Lin Zexu destroys British opium in Canton | Immediate trigger of First Opium War |

| 1856–1860 | Second Opium War | Britain and France vs. China; opium legalized; additional ports opened; foreign envoys allowed in Beijing |

| c. 1870 | Dutch abolish Culture System | Result of humanitarian pressure; but Dutch East Indies remained under colonial rule |
| 1870–1914 | Europeans invest $10+ billion in Latin America | British investment peak in Argentina, Brazil, Mexico; informal empire at its height |
| 1880s | Missionaries introduce cocoa to Gold Coast | Gold Coast becomes world's largest cocoa producer; transforms West African agriculture |

## Chronological Patterns to Know

**Before 1840:** Economic imperialism was primarily driven by trading companies (East India Company, VOC) rather than governments directly. Military force was used, but formal colonization was not always the goal.

**1840–1870:** The Opium Wars establish the "unequal treaty" model in Asia. Industrial military technology decisively tips the balance. British informal empire in Latin America expands through investment.

**1870–1900:** Economic imperialism accelerates globally. The Culture System ends in Indonesia. Egypt is occupied. US corporations begin dominating Central America. The Gold Coast cocoa economy emerges.

**After 1900:** Economic imperialism is fully integrated with political imperialism in Africa and Asia, while in Latin America it remains primarily informal, backed by US government power (Monroe Doctrine, later Roosevelt Corollary).
