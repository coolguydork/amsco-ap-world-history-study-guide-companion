# Cross-Topic Connections — Unit 4.2: (4.2): Exploration

#apwh #unit-4 #period-2

---

## 1. Builds On (Earlier Units)

- **[[Technological Innovations]]** ([[Unit-4.1]]) — Every exploration voyage depended directly on the caravel, compass, and astrolabe developed in 4.1.
- **[[Ottoman Empire and Trade Routes]]** (Unit 3) — Ottoman control of eastern Mediterranean routes was the key economic push factor driving European search for oceanic alternatives.
- **[[Reconquista and Iberian Christianity]]** (Unit 3) — The crusading mindset and military class (future conquistadors) were products of centuries of Iberian religious warfare.
- **[[Zheng He's Voyages]]** (Unit 3) — China's withdrawal from exploration left oceanic space for European powers to fill.
- **[[Indian Ocean Trade Network]]** (Unit 2) — The Portuguese deliberately sought to tap into and redirect existing Indian Ocean commerce.

---

## 2. Sets Up (Later Topics and Units)

- **[[Columbian Exchange]]** ([[Unit-4.3]]) — Exploration made the exchange possible; 4.3 traces its biological and demographic consequences.
- **[[Maritime Empires Link Regions]]** ([[Unit-4.4]]) — Conquest and the feitoria model explored here become full empires in 4.4.
- **[[Atlantic Slave Trade]]** ([[Unit-4.5]]) — Portuguese exploration of West Africa directly established the relationships that fueled the slave trade.
- **[[Atlantic Revolutions]]** (Unit 5) — Spanish colonial rule established in 4.2 is the system that creoles revolt against in Unit 5.
- **[[Decolonization]]** (Unit 9) — The colonial structures created here are ultimately dismantled in the 20th century.

---

## 3. Comparative Cases

- **Spanish vs. Portuguese Empires** — Territorial/tributary vs. trading-post; compare governance models, labor systems, and long-term legacies.
- **European vs. Chinese Exploration (1405–1433 vs. 1488–1600)** — Similar capabilities, radically different political decisions and outcomes.
- **Columbus's "discovery" vs. Norse Contact (c. 1000 CE)** — Norse reached North America earlier but without lasting consequences; why did 1492 matter in a way 1000 CE did not? (State sponsorship, commercial networks, printing press diffusion of knowledge.)
- **Spanish Reconquista vs. Spanish Conquest of Americas** — Same warrior class, same religious justification, different continent.

> [!exam-tip]
> The comparison between **Portuguese and Spanish empire-building** is one of the most frequently tested AP World History comparisons. Know the feitoria vs. encomienda contrast cold.
