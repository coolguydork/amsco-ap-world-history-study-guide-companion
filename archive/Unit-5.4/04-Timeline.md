# Timeline — Unit 5.4: Industrialization Spreads

#apwh #unit-5 #period-3

> **Big Picture:** British industrialization began spreading to Europe and North America from the 1820s. By the 1870s–1880s, Germany and the United States were challenging British industrial supremacy. By 1900, Japan had industrialized in a single generation. China had not. By 1914, the industrial gap between the "core" and the "periphery" had become the primary structural feature of the world economy.

> [!tip] Continuity vs. Change
> **Continuity:** Agricultural economies remained dominant in most of the world through 1900; industrialization was geographically concentrated and left vast regions as raw material suppliers.
> **Change:** The *rate* of economic growth in industrialized nations was genuinely unprecedented; the gap between industrialized and non-industrialized regions widened dramatically, setting the stage for 20th-century global inequality.
