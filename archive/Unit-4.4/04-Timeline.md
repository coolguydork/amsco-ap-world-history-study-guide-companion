# Timeline — Unit 4.4: (4.4): Maritime Empires Link Regions

#apwh #unit-4 #period-2

> [!tip] Big Picture
> Topic 4.4 covers the *establishment* phase of maritime empires — roughly 1488–1565. These decades saw the most dramatic territorial and commercial expansion in European history to that point, fueled by the technologies of 4.1 and the motivations of 4.2.

---

| Date | Event | Region | Significance |
|------|--------|--------|--------------|
| 1497–1499 | Vasco da Gama reaches India | Indian Ocean | Portuguese begin systematic trading-post empire in Indian Ocean |
| 1500 | Portugal claims Brazil | South America | Establishes Portuguese foothold in the Americas; later center of sugar economy |
| 1505–1515 | Albuquerque establishes feitorias at Goa, Malacca, Hormuz | Indian Ocean | Portuguese Estado da India controls three key trade choke points |
| 1519–1521 | Cortés conquers Aztec Empire | Mesoamerica | Spain establishes territorial empire in the most populous part of the Americas |
| 1524 | Spain establishes Council of the Indies | Spain | Central administrative body for colonial governance; represents Crown's attempt at control |
| 1530s | Casa de Contratación regulates all Spanish-American trade | Spain / Americas | Mercantilist control of colonial commerce through Seville |
| 1532 | Pizarro conquers Inca Empire | Andes | Spain gains control of South America's wealthiest region |
| 1535 | Viceroyalty of New Spain established | Mesoamerica | First permanent viceroyalty; administrative model for Spanish America |
| 1542 | New Laws attempt to limit encomienda | Spain / Americas | Crown response to las Casas's advocacy; largely unenforced but signals tension |
| 1542 | Viceroyalty of Peru established | South America | Second major viceroyalty; oversees Andean silver mining |
| 1545 | Silver discovered at Potosí | Bolivia | Foundation of Spanish South American colonial economy |
| 1565 | Spain establishes Manila; Manila Galleon trade begins | Philippines | Spanish empire reaches Asia; first transpacific commercial route |

---

> [!warning] Continuity Note
> The Spanish empire built on existing Indigenous imperial structures — the Aztec tributary network and Inca road system were repurposed for Spanish extraction, not rebuilt from scratch. This is a key continuity from pre-conquest to colonial periods.
