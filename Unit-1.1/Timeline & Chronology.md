# Timeline & Chronology — Topic 1.1: Developments in East Asia
*AMSCO pp. 1–14 | Period 1: c. 1200–c. 1450*

---

## Pre-Period Background (before 1200)

| Date | Event | Significance |
|------|-------|-------------|
| 221 BCE | Qin Dynasty establishes imperial bureaucracy | Continuity: bureaucracy persists through all later dynasties |
| 2nd c. CE | Paper invented in China | Foundation for later woodblock printing |
| 618–907 | Tang Dynasty | Built roads, canals, Grand Canal; promoted trade and Buddhism; later suppressed Buddhism |
| 794–1185 | Heian period in Japan | Japanese court absorbs Chinese culture; *The Tale of Genji* composed |
| c. 9th c. | Woodblock printing developed in China | Expanded literacy; spread scripture and agricultural manuals |
| 960 | Song Dynasty founded by Song Taizu | Restores centralized rule after Tang collapse |
| Before 11th c. | Champa rice adopted in China | Fast-ripening rice drives agricultural revolution and population growth |
| c. 11th c. | *The Tale of Genji* composed by Lady Murasaki Shikibu | World's first novel; Japan's cultural creativity alongside sinification |

---

## Period 1: c. 1200–c. 1450 (Core AP Window)

| Date | Event | Significance |
|------|-------|-------------|
| c. 960–1280 | Neo-Confucianism develops | Syncretic fusion of Confucianism, Daoism, Buddhism; spreads across East Asia |
| 1185 | Heian period ends in Japan | Decline of court culture; feudalism rises |
| 1192 | Minamoto Yoritomo installed as first shogun | Kamakura Shogunate established; emperor becomes figurehead; feudal era begins |
| 1206 | Delhi Sultanate established in India | Regional context: Islamic state rises on China's periphery |
| 1215 | Magna Carta signed in England | Regional context: European political decentralization (compare to Song centralization) |
| 1258 | Mongols conquer Baghdad; Abbasid Caliphate falls | Mongol expansion reaches Islamic world (see Mongol Empire Overview, Topic 2.2) |
| 1271 | Yuan Dynasty declared by Kublai Khan | Mongols begin formal rule of China |
| 1279 | Song Dynasty falls | Mongol conquest complete; Kublai Khan rules all China |
| 1324 | Mansa Musa's pilgrimage to Mecca | Regional context: Mali's wealth and Islam in Africa (see Africa Overview, Topic 1.5) |
| c. 1333 | Kamakura Shogunate ends | Japan's feudal instability continues |
| 1368 | Zhu Yuanzhang founds Ming Dynasty | Expels Mongols; restores native Chinese rule and Confucian governance |
