## Chronological Patterns for AP

**Wave 1 (1945–1955):** South/Southeast Asia — India, Pakistan, Vietnam, Indonesia. Often involved violence and Cold War entanglement.

**Wave 2 (1956–1965):** Africa — Ghana leads, then the "Year of Africa" (1960), then Algeria. Mix of negotiated and armed paths.

**Wave 3 (1965–1975):** Last holdouts — Portuguese Africa (Angola, Mozambique), Zimbabwe. Most violent; Cold War proxy dimensions most pronounced.

> [!exam-tip]
> The AP frequently uses timelines and graphs to ask about the **pace of decolonization**. Know that most African decolonization happened in a 15-year window (1957–1975) and that this speed was itself a cause of instability — new states had little time to build institutions.
