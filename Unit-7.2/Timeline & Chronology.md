## Chronological Narrative

The path to WWI unfolded in two layers. The **structural layer** accumulated over decades: the Franco-Prussian War's aftermath (1870–71) left France bitter and revanchist; the Triple Alliance (1882) and Triple Entente (1907) divided Europe into two armed camps; colonial competition in Africa and Asia created overlapping grievances; and a continuous arms race — especially the Anglo-German naval rivalry — built massive military machines in search of purpose.

The **crisis layer** compressed into five weeks in the summer of 1914. Princip's shots on June 28 gave Austria-Hungary the pretext it had been looking for to crush Serbian nationalism. Its ultimatum was designed to fail. Once Serbia rejected key terms (July 25), mobilization orders followed like dominoes: Austria declared war July 28, Russia mobilized for Serbia, Germany mobilized against Russia, France activated its Russian alliance, and Germany's Schlieffen Plan — invading France via Belgium — finally pulled Britain in on August 4.

> [!exam-tip]
> Know the *sequence* of declarations for SAQ context questions: Austria-Hungary → Russia mobilizes → Germany → France → Britain. The cascade took under six weeks from assassination to a four-power war. The speed illustrates how alliance obligations reduced leaders' room for diplomacy.
