## Connections to Earlier Units

- **[[Silk Roads and Indian Ocean Trade]]** (Unit 2) — Predecessors to the global trade networks of Unit 4; the Indian Ocean world that Europeans entered in 4.2–4.4 was already deeply established.
- **[[Mongol Pax and Technology Diffusion]]** (Unit 2–3) — Spread the technologies (compass, gunpowder) Europeans deployed in Unit 4.
- **[[Land-Based Empires]]** (Unit 3) — The Aztec, Inca, Ottoman, and Chinese empires are the context and foil for maritime empire-building.
- **[[Black Death]]** (Unit 2) — Precursor and comparator for the Columbian Exchange epidemics of 4.3.

## Connections to Later Units

- **[[Atlantic Revolutions]]** (Unit 5) — Direct product of Unit 4: creole identity, slavery, plantation economy, mercantilist resentment.
- **[[Industrialization]]** (Unit 5–6) — Funded by Atlantic slave trade profits; driven by plantation demand for manufactured goods; structurally enabled by joint-stock capitalism.
- **[[Imperialism in Asia and Africa]]** (Unit 6) — The trading-post empire model of Unit 4 evolves into full territorial imperialism.
- **[[Global Migration]]** (Unit 6–9) — The patterns of migration established in Unit 4 (voluntary European emigration, forced African migration) shape global demographic patterns through the modern era.
- **[[Decolonization]]** (Unit 9) — The colonial structures built in Unit 4 are the ones ultimately dismantled in the 20th century.
