# Unit 4.8: Continuity and Change from 1450 to 1750

#apwh #unit-4 #period-4

## AP Exam Period
**Period 4: 1450–1750**

## Summary

Topic 4.8 asks students to step back and analyze the period as a whole: what genuinely changed between 1450 and 1750, and what remained continuous? The period saw transformations of world-historical significance — the first integration of all inhabited continents, the creation of the Atlantic slave trade and the African diaspora, the demographic collapse of Indigenous Americans, the emergence of a global silver economy, and the development of new racial hierarchies. Yet much also persisted: long-distance trade networks continued (though routes shifted); coerced labor systems existed before and continued after this period; states competed for power and resources as they always had; social hierarchies based on class, gender, and ethnicity predated and outlasted colonial rule. Understanding this period requires nuanced CCOT analysis — identifying what changed, at what rate, for whom, and where continuity persisted beneath apparent transformation.

## AMSCO Learning Objectives

- Develop and apply CCOT analysis skills to AP essay prompts spanning this period.
