# Timeline & Chronology — Topic 1.3

*AMSCO pp. 23–32 | Period 1: c. 1200–c. 1450*

## Chronological Overview

| Date | Event | Significance |
|---|---|---|
| c. 500 BCE | Indian merchants reach Southeast Asia | Beginning of Indian cultural diffusion into the region |
| c. 3rd c. BCE | Buddhism arrives in Sri Lanka | Sinhala dynasties become center of Buddhist learning |
| 550 CE | Gupta Dynasty collapses | End of India's "Golden Age"; political fragmentation begins; Rajput kingdoms emerge |
| 670–1025 | **Srivijaya Empire** (Sumatra) | Hindu sea-based kingdom; taxed India-China trade routes |
| 700s CE | First Southeast Asian Muslim converts | Local merchants convert to trade with Muslim partners |
| 700s CE | Islamic armies enter Pakistan | Islam's first entry into South Asia; limited cultural impact due to geographic isolation |
| c. 802–1431 | **Khmer Empire / Angkor Kingdom** | Most powerful land-based kingdom in Southeast Asia; built Angkor Thom and Angkor Wat |
| c. 850–1267 | **Chola Dynasty** (Southern India) | 400+ years of stability; extended to Ceylon; projected naval power into Southeast Asia |
| 11th century | Islamic forces plunder Hindu temples and Buddhist shrines in northern India | Escalation of Muslim military presence; weakening of Buddhism's institutional base |
| 1025 | Srivijaya Empire ends | Sea-lane control passes to successors |
| c. 1181–1218 | Jayavarman VII rules Khmer Empire | Converts Khmer from Hinduism to Buddhism; builds Angkor Thom |
| Early 13th c. | **Delhi Sultanate** established (1206) | First major Muslim state governing South Asia; ruled ~300 years |
| 12th c. onward | **Bhakti Movement** spreads | Hindu mystical reform movement; open to women and low castes; parallel to Sufism |
| c. 1293–1520 | **Majapahit Kingdom** (Java) | Buddhist sea-based empire; peak of 98 tributaries |
| c. 1320–1392 | Lal Ded (Mother Lalla) | Kashmiri poet; embodies Hindu-Sufi cross-cultural dialogue |
| c. 1336–1646 | **Vijayanagara Empire** (Southern India) | Last major Hindu kingdom in the south; founded by reconverted brothers from Delhi Sultanate |
| 1431 | Sukhothai Kingdom invades Khmer | Khmer capital at Angkor abandoned; end of Angkor civilization |
| 1526 | Delhi Sultanate falls to Mughals | Mughals (tracing Mongol ancestry) replace Delhi Sultanate as Muslim rulers of northern India |
| Mid-1500s | Vijayanagara Empire falls | Muslim coalition defeats last major Hindu kingdom in southern India |

## Key Relationship to AP Period 1 (c. 1200–c. 1450)

The AP exam period of 1200–1450 sits in the **middle of most of this topic's timeline**. The Chola Dynasty was already in decline, the Delhi Sultanate was newly established, the Khmer Empire was at its peak and then fell, and the Majapahit Kingdom rose. Focus exam answers on **what is happening during 1200–1450**, not on earlier context (except as contextualization in LEQs).

> [!exam-tip]
> The dates for Southeast Asian kingdoms are tricky — Srivijaya ends in 1025 (just **before** Period 1), and Majapahit spans most of it. Know which kingdoms are active during 1200–1450: Delhi Sultanate, Vijayanagara, Majapahit, Khmer (until 1431), Sinhala dynasties.

> [!tip]
> **Memory anchor:** "Delhi 1206, [[Concept Notes/Mongols\|Mongols]] blocked, Mughals 1526." The Delhi Sultanate begins, successfully fends off the Mongols (unlike many others), then falls to a Mongol-descended dynasty. This sequence also connects to Topic 1.7 (Mongol Empire).
