# Timeline & Chronology

**Topic 8.2 — The Cold War | AMSCO pp. 554–561**

---

| Date | Event | Significance |
|------|--------|--------------|
| 1944–1945 | Yalta and Potsdam Conferences | Allies negotiate postwar order; US-Soviet tensions emerge over Eastern Europe |
| 1945 | United Nations established | Replaces League of Nations; US and USSR both founding members despite rivalry |
| Mar. 1946 | Churchill's "Iron Curtain" speech | Publicly announces Cold War division of Europe; signals end of wartime alliance |
| 1946 | George Kennan's "Long Telegram" | Articulates containment as US Cold War strategy |
| 1947 | Truman Doctrine announced | US commits to supporting nations resisting communist expansion (Greece, Turkey) |
| Jun. 1947 | Marshall Plan proposed | $13 billion US aid program to rebuild Western Europe and prevent communist revolution |
| 1947 | COMECON established (Soviet response) | Soviet economic bloc for Eastern Europe; modest impact compared to Marshall Plan |
| 1947–1948 | Berlin Blockade and Airlift | Soviets blockade West Berlin; US/UK airlift supplies for 11 months; first major Cold War confrontation |
| 1949 | NATO formed | US-led Western military alliance; first peacetime alliance in US history |
| Aug. 1949 | Soviet Union tests atomic bomb | Ends US nuclear monopoly; escalates arms race |
| Oct. 1949 | People's Republic of China founded | Mao's communist victory shocks US; "Who lost China?" debate |
| 1950–1953 | Korean War | First major Cold War proxy conflict; UN intervention; ends in stalemate at 38th parallel |
| 1955 | Bandung Conference | 29 African/Asian nations meet in Indonesia; condemn colonialism; precursor to Non-Aligned Movement |
| Oct. 1957 | Soviet Union launches Sputnik | First artificial satellite; begins Space Race; demonstrates Soviet ICBM capability |
| Jan. 1958 | US launches Explorer 1 | American response to Sputnik; NASA created same year |
| 1957 | Both sides test ICBMs | Mutual Assured Destruction becomes operational reality |
| 1961 | Non-Aligned Movement formally founded | Belgrade, Yugoslavia; 25 founding members |
| Aug. 1961 | Berlin Wall constructed | Seals East Germany; symbol of Cold War division |
| Oct. 1962 | Cuban Missile Crisis | Closest the Cold War came to nuclear war; resolved by Soviet withdrawal of missiles |

---

> [!exam-tip]
> **Three pivotal years for AP:** 1947 (Truman Doctrine + Marshall Plan = containment in action), 1949 (Soviet bomb + PRC = escalation), 1962 (Cuban Missile Crisis = peak danger). Knowing these clusters helps structure chronological arguments.

> [!tip]
> The **Berlin timeline** is a useful running thread: Potsdam (1945) divides Berlin → Berlin Blockade (1947–48) tests Western resolve → Berlin Wall (1961) physically seals the division. It shows the Cold War's intensification in concrete physical form.
