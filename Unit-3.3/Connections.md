## AP Threads Running Through This Topic

**Religion and state legitimacy** — Every empire in this era used religion to justify political authority. Rulers who controlled religion controlled legitimacy. This thread runs from the Ottomans (shariah) through the Safavids (Shia imam-king) through the Mughal (divine-right tolerance) to European monarchs (head of church).

**Religious conflict and tolerance** — The spectrum from Akbar's active tolerance to Ismail's forced conversion captures the full range. The Reformation wars show that tolerance was hard-won, not assumed.

**Syncretism vs. orthodoxy** — Sikhism (organic synthesis from below) vs. Din-i-Ilahi (imposed synthesis from above) vs. Calvinist Geneva (strict orthodoxy) illustrates the creative tension between religious blending and religious purity. See [[Concept Notes/Syncretism|Syncretism]].

**New religious movements** — Sikhism joins Buddhism, Christianity, and Islam as religions that emerged from existing traditions in new cultural contexts. Pattern: new religions tend to emerge at cultural contact zones.
