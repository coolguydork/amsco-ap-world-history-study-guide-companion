# Unit 2.7: Timeline — Comparison of Economic Exchange

#apwh #unit-2 #period-1

> **Big picture:** This timeline places all three major trade networks side by side across c. 1200–1450, revealing that they were not separate systems but an interconnected web — disruptions in one reverberated through the others. The Mongol conquests simultaneously devastated the Silk Roads and enabled them; the plague that killed Silk Road merchants also sailed into Mediterranean ports on Genoese ships; the Trans-Saharan gold that funded Islamic civilization was the same gold that reached Europe through Italian intermediaries. The period ends with these networks intact but shifting — setting the stage for European maritime efforts to bypass them entirely.
