## Chronological Patterns to Know

**Arab-Israeli Wars sequence:** 1948 (independence/refugee crisis) → 1956 (Suez) → 1967 (Six-Day War, territorial expansion) → 1973 (Yom Kippur, oil embargo) → 1978 (Camp David). Each war changed territorial realities and radicalized both sides.

**South Asian independence pattern:** 1947 (partition and violence) → 1948 (Kashmir War) → 1965 (second Kashmir War) → 1971 (Bangladesh War) → 1974 (India nuclear test) → 1998 (both nuclear). Trajectory: from colonial partition to nuclear standoff.

**Cambodia trajectory:** 1953 independence → Cold War neutrality → 1970 coup → drawn into Vietnam War → 1975 Khmer Rouge genocide → 1979 Vietnamese intervention → 1991 UN peace → democracy.

**Africa:** 1961 Tanganyika independence → 1964 Tanzania formed → 1967 Arusha Declaration → 1985 Nyerere resigns. Pattern: idealistic socialist project → economic difficulties → peaceful transition.

> [!exam-tip]
> The AP exam frequently asks you to trace **causation across time**. Practice connecting: 1917 Balfour Declaration → 1947 UN partition → 1948 refugee crisis → 1967 Six-Day War → 1973 oil embargo → 1978 Camp David. Each event caused or enabled the next.
